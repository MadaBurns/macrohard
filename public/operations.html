Operations — the restructuring desk

Ten gates. None of them can hand you an error.

Spend is the design constraint here, and the desk is the only part of the company that can spend money. What follows is the whole path a request takes, in order. Each gate either refuses you in one sentence, or gives you something readable and says what it is.

GateIf it does not pass
01 Shape · 3–200 characters, no links Refuse “Words, not links.”
02 Cache · 30 days, keyed on your sentence Skip A hit skips all eight gates below.
03 Turnstile · off unless a secret is set Refuse “Please complete the check.”
04 Per-visitor rate · six a minute Refuse “Slow down — six a minute is plenty.”
05 Daily cap · a fixed number of model calls to the UTC day Degrade A standing proposal, labelled. Your text is not stored. Note 8.5.
06 Guard, inbound · reads your description Refuse “Not that one.”
07 Generate · strict schema, then scrubbed of anything link-shaped Degrade The closest standing proposal, under your own title.
08 Guard, outbound · reads the chart it produced, in context Degrade As above.
09 Permalink · needs both guard passes to have answered Degrade You get the chart, but no link. Nothing is stored, cached or counted.
10 Share card · one render per link, behind a lock Degrade The site's own card. The link still works, and always will.

Why only one gate carries a figure

Gates 05 and 09 are the two that change what you are given without refusing you, and they are marked above. Only the cap keeps a running count; the other nine are decided and forgotten inside a single request. Counting them would cost a write per request per gate. Note 4 says an estimate is not a receipt, so the rest carry no number rather than a guessed one.

Where the numbers live

The exact ceilings are configuration, not copy, so this page does not restate them: it would be one more place for them to go stale. They are in wrangler.jsonc in the public repository, beside the reasoning for each one, and the current day's spend against the cap is reported live in the band at the top of the front page.
